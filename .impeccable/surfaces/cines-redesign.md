# Cines.com.py redesign surface brief

- **Mode:** Operate. Visitors are here to find a film, cinema and session, not to read a marketing pitch.
- **Audience:** Cinema-goers in Paraguay, with mobile-first use and quick comparison across locations.
- **Task:** Search current films, inspect raw published sessions, identify the cinema and continue to the official source.
- **Proof:** Movie names, posters, genres, cinema lists and showtime strings from the panel's JSON feed; other facts only from verified official pages.
- **Constraints:** Static-only hosting, SEO-first HTML, no custom backend, no fabricated detail, six-hour rebuild, independent proposal disclosure.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A usable cinema programme for Paraguay, not a clone of a generic streaming catalogue. It refuses the poster-wall-plus-rounded-card default by treating the page as a printed programme translated into a highly legible digital wayfinding system.

**OWN-WORLD:** Ink-dark petrol, mineral paper and a single phosphor-lime accent; dense but calm programme typography; clear numbering and thin registration rules; a ticket-edge cut used only to separate schedule groups. No stock film-reel decoration or fake marquee lights.

**STORY:** Visitors understand what is showing, where it is playing and which showtime text belongs to which cinema, then follow a verified link to the original listing.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A compact masthead and navigation sit above a two-column programme opener: the current lead film and its large source poster on one side, a short list of other current films and a cinema-session prompt on the other. The first viewport shows real content, not a vague slogan or decorative hero.

**FORM:** Editorial programme sheet with wayfinding hierarchy and restrained ticket-stub cuts. Concept-seed CLI unavailable in this runtime; `seed: none (unavailable)` rather than inventing a seed or claiming the command ran.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

## Unresolved
Cinema detail URLs, full addresses, ticket prices, film runtimes, age ratings, cast, synopsis and structured upcoming-release data are not present in the panel feed. They must be added only from verified sources.
